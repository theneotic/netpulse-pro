Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Overview

NetPulse Pro is an open, browser-side network telemetry tool designed with privacy as a foundational principle. We do not collect, track, or sell any personal data or browsing behavior.

2. Data Storage

All test measurements (ping, jitter, download speed, and upload speed) are stored strictly within your browser's local storage (localStorage). No test history is ever uploaded to any central server or database. You have full control to clear your history or export it (CSV/JSON) at any time.

3. Network Telemetry Requests

When you trigger a test, your browser sends speed test probes directly to Cloudflare’s public edge endpoints (speed.cloudflare.com). These requests are governed by Cloudflare’s standard privacy policies and edge network routing.

4. Cookies and Tracking

NetPulse Pro does not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or third-party advertising scripts.

5. Contact

For inquiries or source inspection, visit our official repository or contact the project maintainers.
